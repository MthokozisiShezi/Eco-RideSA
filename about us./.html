About Us

Who we are and why we started EcoRide SA.

Our Story

EcoRide SA started in January 2025. Two engineering students from the University of Johannesburg were tired of the hassle of getting around for short trips, so they decided to start their own scooter rental business.

They were inspired by the electric scooters that had already appeared in Cape Town and other cities overseas.

We began with 20 scooters at Randburg CBD, Cresta and UJ Kingsway Campus. Today we have 80 scooters and we launched our website in 2026.

Our Mission

To give students and young professionals a cheap, simple and eco-friendly way to travel short distances.

Our Vision

To be the first choice for short trips in Gauteng, with scooter stations in more areas.

Our Values

  • Clear prices
  • Rider safety
  • Looking after the environment
